ML-ASSISTED B2B PLATFORM · 2025–2026

Turning industrial expertise into a digital business workflow.

I saw an opportunity to help a manufacturer work more effectively with overseas B2B clients: bring the knowledge behind its past projects into a digital design and procurement process.

MY ROLEProduct & business lead
PROJECT SCALE / OUTCOME~2 months → under 1 monthDesign-to-contract cycle

A business relationship slowed by the process

The manufacturer faced communication and business coordination barriers when working with overseas clients. Much of the process still revolved around trade shows: meeting potential buyers, understanding requirements, and arranging follow-up discussions. That dependence carried substantial time and opportunity costs. I saw it as a wider challenge in traditional manufacturing, where valuable technical experience often remains tied to in-person exchanges.

Finding the value in past projects

I proposed a more efficient digital route and worked to understand the company’s requirements and historical cases. Those cases contained experience that could inform new projects. The question was how to organize that knowledge digitally so it could be used alongside a new client’s site constraints, layout preferences, and market requirements.

Learning from the people who make projects possible

I conducted in-depth interviews with engineers and geological survey specialists to understand the technical judgments behind project decisions. These conversations connected the commercial problem with the expertise needed to configure a viable project. They informed how I translated business requirements and historical experience into the website’s automated workflow.

Designing automation the business could oversee

As product and business lead, I worked across client discovery, system design, engineering coordination, and deployment testing. Our team built an ML-assisted design and procurement platform that used historical cases and project requirements to recommend site-adapted configurations. I focused on making that automation understandable and subject to business oversight, so the people using it could retain judgment over the process.

A shorter path from design to agreement

With the platform, the cross-border design and contract-signing cycle moved from roughly two months to under one month. The project brought industry experience into a digital workflow and gave the business a more efficient way to develop project configurations with overseas clients.
