LLM-Assisted ERP

Internal ERP for 10 cross-border e-commerce firms, with human approval kept for consequential actions.

  • Role Product & business lead, 5-person engineering team
  • Timeline Dec 2025 – present
  • Users 10 overseas-facing e-commerce firms

Problem

Existing ERP tooling required substantial manual monitoring, coordination, and data entry, while overseas sales created time-zone pressure and irregular working hours. The question was whether an LLM-based system could support real operational decisions — not just automate tasks — while staying understandable and controllable.

Approach

The system monitors sales, detects operational problems, forecasts inventory risks, and supports SKU-related actions. Consequential actions stay behind approval steps. A domain knowledge base — entrepreneur networks, firms' development paths, regional policy, trade conditions — gives the system context beyond each firm's own history.

What I did

Client discovery, workflow mapping, product design, development coordination and testing. Translated operating problems into requirements and encoded company-specific decision rules (cash constraints, supplier lead times, inventory policies) into the system.
