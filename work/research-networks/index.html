Research Networks & Lab Organization

How humanities and social science labs form, collaborate, and integrate knowledge across boundaries.

  • Role Research assistant, National Social Science Fund Main Project (24&ZD292)
  • Timeline Feb 2024 – Aug 2026
  • Data 250+ laboratories, 8,000+ scholars

Question

How do research organizations form, collaborate, and integrate knowledge across disciplinary and institutional boundaries as the conditions of knowledge production change?

Approach

Built a reproducible dataset from official public web pages of US humanities and social science labs: university–lab–member structure with source URLs, evidence text, and audit trails. Network analysis of collaboration patterns across the lab as the unit of analysis.

Outputs

Conference paper: "Reorganizing knowledge production: the organizational forms of humanities and social science laboratories in the United States" (Chinese Sociological Association Annual Conference, 2026, Sociology of Technology session).
