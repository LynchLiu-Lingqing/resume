HOW THE PROJECT TOOK SHAPE

The business problem
came first.

Working with a manufacturer serving overseas B2B clients, I saw how much communication and coordination still depended on trade shows. The time and opportunity costs pointed to a problem I saw across traditional manufacturing.

I proposed a digital workflow built around the company’s needs and past projects. Through in-depth interviews with engineers and geological survey specialists, I explored how that experience could inform automated project configuration.

My focus was automation the business could understand and oversee—grounded in its expertise and useful in its day-to-day work.
