ML-Assisted Amusement Design

B2B platform for overseas amusement projects, built with Zhongshan Golden Dragon Group.

  • Role Product & business lead, 5-person engineering team
  • Timeline Mar 2025 – present
  • Outcome Cross-border design & contract cycle cut from ~2 months to under 1 month

Problem

Overseas amusement projects combine site constraints, layout preferences, and market requirements that differ sharply across regions. Traditional design and procurement cycles took roughly two months, with heavy manual coordination across borders.

Approach

The platform combines historical project cases, site constraints, layout preferences, and market requirements to recommend site-adapted project configurations. ML assists configuration search; human review stays in the loop for consequential decisions.

What I did

Led problem definition and client discovery, translated operating needs into requirements, coordinated development and deployment testing, and worked with the client on rollout.
